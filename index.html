GAME KNOWLEDGE

Rules & Strategy

A practical reference for understanding competitive table tennis.
01 / SCORING

How scoring works

A standard game is played to 11 points. A player must win by at least two points. At 10–10, play continues until one player leads by two points.

02 / SERVICE

Legal service

The ball must rest freely on the open palm of the stationary free hand. It is thrown nearly vertically upward and struck as it falls.

03 / ALTERNATION

Service rotation

Service changes every two points. When the score reaches 10–10, service changes after every point.

04 / LET

Let and replay

A rally may be replayed when a qualifying interruption occurs, such as a serve touching the net and otherwise being legal.

05 / DOUBLES

Doubles rotation

In doubles, players serve diagonally and teammates must alternate strokes during a rally.

06 / EQUIPMENT

Racket requirements

The racket must have a flat, rigid blade. Its covering must meet the applicable competition regulations.
