Is DANESH SPIN suitable for beginners? +

Absolutely! DANESH SPIN is designed for all skill levels. We start from the very basics and progressively build up to advanced techniques, ensuring everyone has a clear learning path.

How often should I practice? +

We recommend practicing 3-4 times per week for at least 45 minutes. Consistency is more important than duration. Use our practice timer to structure your sessions!

What equipment do I need to start? +

To get started, you only need a basic racket, a few balls, and a table. Check out our Gear Shop for our top recommendations at every price point.

Can I compete professionally? +

Yes! With dedication and our tournament-ready training program, many of our users have gone on to compete at regional and national levels.

Is the content free? +

The core content is completely free. We also offer premium features for serious players who want personalized coaching and advanced analytics.
