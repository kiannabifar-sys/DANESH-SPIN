KNOW YOUR SETUP

EQUIPMENT GUIDE

Understand the main parts of table tennis equipment and how they influence playing style.

🏓
THE FOUNDATION

BLADES

The blade is the wooden or composite base of a racket. Its feel, speed and control depend on its construction.

🔴
CONTACT & SPIN

RUBBERS

Rubber surfaces influence grip, spin, speed and control. Different styles suit different techniques.

⚪
THE GAME OBJECT

TABLE TENNIS BALLS

Modern competition balls are plastic and typically marked 40+. Consistency and condition matter.

👟
MOVEMENT

SHOES

Table tennis footwear should support comfortable movement, grip and stability on the playing surface.

👕
COMFORT

APPAREL

Comfortable sports clothing helps you move freely and stay focused during practice and matches.

🧳
CARE & STORAGE

RACKET CARE

Keep your racket clean, protect the rubber surface and store equipment in a suitable case.

RULES & KNOWLEDGE

A few essential facts to help you understand the sport more deeply.

📏

THE TABLE

A standard table is 2.74 m long, 1.525 m wide and 76 cm high. The net divides it into two playing courts.

🔢

SCORING

A standard game is played to 11 points. A player must win by at least two points. Service changes every two points, then alternates every point at 10–10.

🎾

THE SERVICE

The ball must rest freely on the open palm before being tossed upward. The server must strike it so it first touches their court and then the receiver's court.

🌍

THE INTERNATIONAL GAME

The International Table Tennis Federation (ITTF) is the sport's international governing body and publishes official rules and competition information.

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Can I use this website without an account?

Yes. You can explore the learning content, spin visual, timer, quiz and other tools without signing in.

Where is my progress saved?

Your personal progress is stored in this browser using local storage. Clearing browser data may remove it, so export a backup regularly.

Does this website provide real online multiplayer?

No. This version is an educational and personal tracking platform. Real-time multiplayer needs a server and networking system.

How can I keep my records?

Use the backup tools below to export your records as a JSON file. You can import that file later in this browser.

Can I use it on a phone?

Yes. The layout adapts to smaller screens and touch devices.
