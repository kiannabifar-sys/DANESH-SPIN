RULES QUICK GUIDE

Essential rules

Games are normally played to 11 points.
A player must win by at least two points.
Service changes every two points, then every point at 10–10.
The serve must begin with the ball resting on the open palm.
In singles, the serve can go to any part of the opponent's court.

23 — HELP DESK

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Does the website have real online player accounts?

This version saves a local profile in your browser. Real accounts and cloud synchronisation require a backend and database.

Can I access my records on another device?

Use the export backup feature on the data management section and import the file on another device. Automatic cloud sync is not included.

Does the reaction test measure professional reaction ability?

No. It is a simple browser interaction. Screen refresh rate, input device and browser performance can affect the result.

Can I use this website on a phone?

Yes. The layout is responsive and adapts to smaller screens.

Are the training drills personalised coaching?

They are general educational practice ideas. A qualified coach can adapt exercises to your individual technique and needs.
